Mechanical Maintenance Unit

Equipment

Tala Hydropower Plant

Overview

The Mechanical Maintenance Unit is responsible for maintenance of major mechanical systems and equipment associated with the generating units.

Hydro Turbine System

Pelton Turbine

The generating units are equipped with high-head Pelton turbines. The turbine system consists of runner, nozzle assemblies, deflectors and associated mechanical components.

Main Components

  • Runner
  • Nozzle Assembly
  • Deflector System
  • Brake System

Main Inlet Valve (MIV)

The Main Inlet Valve is a spherical valve installed upstream of the turbine. It controls water flow to the turbine and provides isolation during maintenance activities.

  • Service Seal
  • Maintenance Seal
  • Bypass System

  • Heat Exchangers
