About Mechanical Maintenance Unit

The Mechanical Maintenance Unit is responsible for the maintenance, inspection, troubleshooting and reliability improvement of mechanical systems and equipment at Tala Hydropower Plant.

The unit ensures safe and reliable operation of hydro-mechanical equipment through planned maintenance, condition monitoring, and continuous improvement practices.
